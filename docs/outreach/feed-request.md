# Request for a public calendar feed (clubs, career centres, companies)

Use this for sources whose pages we can't read: JavaScript-only pages, pages behind a login, or sites whose terms need permission.

**Subject:** Could OpenRoster list your [club / office / company] events? (free student tracker)

Hi [team name],

I run OpenRoster (https://github.com/jayshah2005/OpenRoster), a free, open-source site that helps computer science students in Ontario find hackathons, info sessions and career events in one place. We'd love to include your [events / info sessions / student programs].

The easiest way is a **public events page or calendar feed**: an events page on your site, a Luma calendar, or an RSS or iCal feed (Google Calendar feeds do not work for us, because Google's robots.txt does not allow automated reading). We read it at most every 6 hours and show only the facts (title, date, place, registration link), always linking back to you. We never copy descriptions or images, and we remove anything within 48 hours on request.

If a feed isn't possible, you can also submit events through our form: https://github.com/jayshah2005/OpenRoster/issues/new?template=submit-opportunity.yml
